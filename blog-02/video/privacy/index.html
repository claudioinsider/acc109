The following Privacy Policy governs the online information collection practices of ClearGaze ("we" or "us"). Specifically, it outlines the types of information that we gather about you while you are using this website (the "Site"), and the ways in which we use this information. This Privacy Policy applies only to information we collect through the Site and does not apply to our collection of information from other sources.

By visiting and/or using our Site, you agree that your use of our Site, and any dispute over privacy, is governed by this Privacy Policy. We may need to change this Privacy Policy from time to time; whenever we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

How We Collect and Use Information

We may collect and store personal or other information that you voluntarily supply to us online while using the Site (e.g., while registering or placing an order), including your name, email address, postal address and payment information. We also collect and store information that is generated automatically as you navigate online through the Site, such as information about your connection.

We use cookies — small files that your web browser places on your hard drive for record-keeping purposes — to enhance your experience on the Site and to track usage patterns. We may also use web beacons to count the number of users who visit the Site.

We use the information we collect to customize features and advertising that appear on the Site and to improve our products and services. Information may be shared with third parties unless you request otherwise by contacting our Online Privacy Coordinator.

Third-party advertisers and ad servers may also collect data through cookies or web beacons. Their use of such data is governed by their own privacy policies, not by this one. The Site also contains links to other websites whose privacy practices we do not control.

Be aware that we may occasionally release information about our visitors when release is appropriate to comply with law or to protect the rights, property or safety of users of the Site or the public. If our company or its assets are sold, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics Advertiser Features to optimize our business. These features include: Remarketing with Google Analytics, Google Display Network Impression Reporting, DoubleClick Platform integrations, and Google Analytics Demographics and Interest Reporting. This allows us to contact you if you begin to fill out our check-out form but abandon it before completion.

You can opt out of these features at any time through Google's Ads Settings or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

Facebook

As advertisers on Facebook, we may collect certain information from users who interact with our ads. We follow Facebook's Data Use Restrictions: we do not use Facebook advertising data for retargeting outside of Facebook's tools, for building or augmenting user profiles, or for transfer to ad networks.

General Data Privacy Regulation (GDPR)

We do not specifically market to the European Union; however, we will comply with the GDPR where it applies. If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you may have the right to request: access to, correction of, deletion of, portability of, and restriction or objection to processing of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at gdpr@getClearGaze.com.

Children's Privacy Statement

This Site is not directed to children under the age of thirteen, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under thirteen. If we discover that such information has been collected, it will be deleted.

How Do We Store Your Information?

Your information is stored at the list server that delivers the Site's content. Only those who manage the lists can access your information, for the purpose of email delivery. You can remove yourself at any time from our mailing list by clicking on the unsubscribe link that can be found in every communication that we send you.

Disclaimer

This policy may be changed at any time at our discretion. Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@getClearGaze.com.
